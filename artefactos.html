💎 GUÍA OFICIAL DE ARTEFACTOS Set 18: Enchanted Wilds

Guía de artefactos del set 18 de TFT: Los mejores artefactos para cada campeón

Artifacts are some of the most powerful items in TFT, but their unusual effects make them much harder to place than standard items. The right Artifact can completely change a champion, while the wrong holder can waste most of its value.

This guide covers the best Artifact holders in TFT Set 18: Enchanted Wilds. Once you have found the right Artifact holder, check out our TFT team comps page for the latest comps and builds for the current patch.
